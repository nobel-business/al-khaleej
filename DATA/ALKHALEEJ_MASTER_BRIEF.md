### About
Established in 2025 in Kafr El-Sheikh, Egypt, Alkhaleej Fish Manufacturing produces fish meal and crude fish oil. We process whole marine fish, including sardines, sourced through our fishing vessel operations and other marine supply channels, into ingredients used in feed manufacturing.

### Products
**Fish Meal:** Marine-origin feed ingredient offered in 60%, 65%, and 72% commercial protein grades; packed in 50 kg bags.
**Crude Fish Oil:** Oil recovered during marine fish processing and supplied in drums for applications suited to its technical specifications.

### Manufacturing
Our process begins with receiving whole marine fish, followed by indirect steam cooking, pressing, and oil–water separation. The press cake proceeds through drying, cooling, and final conditioning before fish meal is packed in 50 kg bags. Crude fish oil is packed in drums.

### Quality
Protein, moisture, fat, and ash are key parameters used to assess fish meal. Available sample measurements cover these indicators during production and for finished-product samples. Verified technical specifications will be provided when approved.

### International Markets
We aim to build supply relationships with feed manufacturers, importers, and distributors across international markets. Contact us to discuss grade, volume, destination, and technical or commercial requirements.
