WHO WE ARE

Rooted in the sea.
Built for industry.

Established in 2025 in Kafr El-Sheikh, Egypt, Alkhaleej Fish Manufacturing specializes in producing fish meal and crude fish oil from whole marine fish, including sardines and other suitable species.

With raw materials sourced through our fishing vessel operations and marine suppliers, we connect the sea with the needs of modern feed manufacturing.
