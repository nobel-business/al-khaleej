## 3. Explicitly prohibited claims (until evidence is supplied)

- Production capacity, tonnage, employee counts.
- Any certification name, logo or "certified/compliant" wording.
- "In-house laboratory", "fully traceable", "sustainably sourced", "100% own fleet", "freshest", "food-grade", "pharmaceutical-grade".
- Completed exports, export volumes, named customer countries, world maps with markers, "global leader", "world-class", "best in class", "Premium" (pending G-21).
- Customer names, logos, testimonials, case studies.
- Any of the eight sample readings or their averages as product specifications.
- Specific temperatures, moisture targets or machinery names from the informal process description.
- Contact details found on mockups (G-01…G-03) until confirmed.
