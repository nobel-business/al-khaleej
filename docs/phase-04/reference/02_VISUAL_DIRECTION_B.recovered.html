01 الشركة

The company

A marine ingredients manufacturer established in 2025 in Kafr El-Sheikh, Egypt.

Alkhaleej Fish Manufacturing produces fish meal and crude fish oil. We process whole marine fish, including sardines, sourced through our fishing vessel operations and other marine supply channels, into ingredients used in feed manufacturing.

We welcome supply inquiries from domestic and international buyers.

05 الجودة

Four parametersالبروتين · الرطوبة · الدهون · الرماد

Proteinالبروتين

The basis of each commercial grade — 60%, 65% or 72%.

Moistureالرطوبة

Shaped by drying and cooling; it matters for storage and transport.

Fatالدهون

Antioxidant is added to help protect fats from oxidation.

Ashالرماد

The mineral fraction, read alongside protein by feed formulators.

Available sample measurements cover these indicators during production and on finished-product samples. Verified technical specifications and certificates of analysis will be provided when approved.
